Basketball — Physical Stats of the Environment

Sources: NBA, WNBA, FIBA, NCAA and NFHS rulebooks and equipment rules. Imperial and metric. Lines marked COMPUTED are calculated from the official figures, not published figures.

1. The Ball — NBA (Size 7)

Circumference: 29.5 in / 74.9 cm — Rule No. 1 (2009-10 edition states the figure explicitly; current editions fix the pressure in Rule 1 and the size via the approved Wilson game ball)

Diameter: 9.39 in / 23.85 cm — COMPUTED (circumference ÷ π)

Weight: 22 oz / 623.7 g — Rule No. 1 (2009-10 edition)

Inflation pressure: 7.5–8.5 psi / 51.7–58.6 kPa — Rule No. 1, Section II(f)

Material / construction: genuine (full-grain) leather cover, 8 panels, pebbled surface — Wilson official game ball spec

Average density: 0.088 g/cm³ / 87.8 kg/m³ — COMPUTED (623.7 g ÷ sphere volume from 74.9 cm circumference, V ≈ 7,104 cm³)

1. The Ball — WNBA (Size 6)

Circumference: 28.5–29 in / 72.4–73.7 cm — WNBA Official Rules 2026, Rule No. 1, Section II(f)(1)

Diameter: 9.07–9.23 in / 23.0–23.5 cm — COMPUTED

Weight: 18–20 oz / 510.3–567.0 g — Rule No. 1, Section II(f)(1)

Inflation pressure: 7 psi / 48.3 kPa (recommended) — Rule No. 1 §II(f)(1) and Rule No. 2 §II(f)

Material / construction: composite leather cover ("micro-touch" composite), 8 panels, orange-and-white panels — Wilson official game ball spec

Average density: 0.076–0.089 g/cm³ / 75.6–88.5 kg/m³ across the legal range; 0.089 g/cm³ / 88.5 kg/m³ at nominal 28.5 in and 20 oz — COMPUTED

1. The Ball — FIBA men (Size 7)

Circumference: 29.49–30.71 in / 749–780 mm — FIBA Basketball Equipment, §7.4

Diameter: 9.39–9.77 in / 23.84–24.83 cm — COMPUTED

Weight: 20.0–22.9 oz / 567–650 g — §7.4

Inflation pressure: no psi value published; set by a bounce test — dropped from 1,800 mm (70.9 in), the ball must rebound to 1,200–1,400 mm (47.2–55.1 in), measured to the top of the ball — §7

Material / construction: leather or artificial/composite/synthetic leather, single shade of orange or FIBA-approved colour combination, spherical, max 12 seams each no wider than 6.35 mm (0.25 in) — §7.3

Average density: 0.071–0.092 g/cm³ / 70.8–91.6 kg/m³ across the legal range; 0.081 g/cm³ / 80.6 kg/m³ at the range midpoints (76.45 cm, 608.5 g) — COMPUTED

1. The Ball — NCAA men (Size 7) and women (Size 6)

Women — weight: 18–20 oz / 510.3–567.0 g

Women — inflation: rebound test — dropped from 6 ft (1.83 m), rebound to 51–56 in (129.5–142.2 cm) at the top of the ball

Women — average density: 0.076–0.089 g/cm³ / 75.6–88.5 kg/m³ across the legal range; 0.082 g/cm³ / 81.9 kg/m³ at the midpoints (28.75 in, 19 oz) — COMPUTED

High school (NFHS, for reference) — circumference: boys 29.5 in / 74.9 cm; girls 28.5 in / 72.4 cm (nominal sizes; NFHS adopts the NCAA-style ranges) — NFHS Rule 1

2. The Court — overall size and surface

Length × width: 94 ft × 50 ft / 28.65 m × 15.24 m — NBA, WNBA, NCAA men & women

Length × width: 91.86 ft × 49.21 ft / 28 m × 15 m, measured from the inner edge of the boundary lines — FIBA, Art. 2

Length × width: 84 ft × 50 ft / 25.60 m × 15.24 m ideal; maximum permitted 94 ft × 50 ft — NFHS, Rule 1-1

Playing area: 4,700 sq ft / 436.6 m² — NBA — COMPUTED (94 × 50)

Playing area: 4,520.8 sq ft / 420 m² — FIBA — COMPUTED (28 × 15)

Playing area: 4,200 sq ft / 390.1 m² — NFHS — COMPUTED (84 × 50, ideal court)

Floor material: wood (wooden flooring mandatory for Level 1 and 2 competition) — FIBA Basketball Equipment

Floor material: not specified in the playing rules (hardwood, in practice maple, is universal but is not a Rule 1 figure) — NBA / NCAA / NFHS

Free-throw semicircle radius: 5.91 ft / 1.80 m, centre at the midpoint of the free-throw line — FIBA

2. The Court — three-point line, lines and run-off

Three-point, NBA: arc 23 ft 9 in / 7.24 m; corner 22 ft / 6.71 m; parallel lines 3 ft / 0.91 m from the sidelines, running 14 ft / 4.27 m from the baseline before meeting the arc

Three-point, WNBA: arc 22 ft 1¾ in / 6.75 m; parallel lines 36 in / 0.91 m from the sidelines, extending 93⅓ in / 2.37 m from the baseline; corner distance 22 ft / 6.71 m where the line passes the ring centre

Three-point, FIBA: arc 22.15 ft / 6.75 m; corner 21.65 ft / 6.60 m; parallel lines 2.95 ft / 0.90 m from the sidelines

Three-point, NCAA men & women: arc 22 ft 1¾ in / 6.75 m to the outside edge of the line; corner 21 ft 7⅞ in / 6.60 m (same distance for both since the women adopted it in 2021-22)

Three-point, NFHS: arc 19 ft 9 in / 6.02 m (semicircle, same distance in the corners; straight extensions 63 in / 1.60 m long from the end line)

Line width: 2 in / 5.08 cm — NBA / WNBA / NCAA / NFHS

Line width: 1.97 in / 50 mm, all lines the same clearly visible colour — FIBA

Clear space beyond the boundary lines: at least 6.56 ft / 2 m from spectators, advertising boards or any obstruction — FIBA

Clear space beyond the boundary lines: at least 3 ft / 0.91 m, preferably 10 ft / 3.05 m — NFHS

3. Hoop Assembly — rim (basket ring)

Height of the upper edge above the floor: 10 ft / 3.05 m — NBA / WNBA / NCAA / NFHS

Height of the top edge above the floor: 10 ft / 3,050 mm, tolerance ±6 mm (0.24 in) — FIBA, §3.4

Inside diameter: 18 in / 45.72 cm — NBA / WNBA / NCAA / NFHS

Inside diameter: 17.72–18.07 in / 450–459 mm — FIBA, §3.1

Material: pressure-release metal safety ring, painted orange — NBA / WNBA

Material / gauge: solid steel, painted orange; the metal rod is 16–20 mm (0.63–0.79 in) in diameter; net attached at 12 places — FIBA, §3.1–3.2

Rod gauge: ring metal no more than ⅝ in / 15.9 mm in diameter — NFHS, Rule 1-10-2

Rod gauge: not published (the rulebook specifies only a "pressure-release metal safety ring") — NBA / WNBA

Gap, nearest point of the inside edge of the ring to the face of the backboard: 6 in / 15.24 cm — NBA / WNBA / NCAA / NFHS

Gap, ring to backboard: 5.94 in / 151 mm — FIBA

Dimensions: 5.91 ft × 3.44 ft / 1,800 mm (+ max 30 mm) × 1,050 mm (+ max 20 mm) — FIBA, §2.3

Material: "transparent" is all the rulebook states (glass in practice; the material is not named) — NBA / WNBA

Shooter's square: 24 in × 18 in / 60.96 cm × 45.72 cm outside dimensions, 2 in / 5.08 cm white line — NBA / WNBA / NCAA / NFHS

Shooter's square: 23.23 in × 17.72 in / 590 mm (+ max 20 mm) × 450 mm (+ max 8 mm) outside dimensions — FIBA

Distance, face of backboard to end line: 4 ft / 1.22 m inside the end line — NBA / WNBA / NCAA / NFHS

Distance, front surface of backboard to end line: 3.94 ft / 1.20 m inside the end line — FIBA

4. Other physical facts

Minimum ceiling height / lowest obstruction: 22.97 ft / 7.00 m above the playing court — FIBA (the only league that publishes one)

Minimum ceiling height: not stated in the playing rules (no official figure published) — NBA / WNBA / NCAA / NFHS

Backboard padding: must cover the bottom and sides of the board to 15 in / 38.1 cm up from the bottom edge — NFHS, Rule 1-9-1

Backboard padding: required on the backstop unit (thickness and coverage set in the equipment rules) — FIBA

Reduced courts for non-major competitions: existing courts may be approved down to 24 m × 13 m (78.74 ft × 42.65 ft) if the change is proportional — FIBA

Mini-basketball ball (Size 5): circumference 27.17–27.95 in / 690–710 mm; weight 16.6–17.6 oz / 470–500 g — FIBA (mini competitions), §7.6

Provenance flag: the NBA's current Rule 1 fixes only the ball pressure (7.5–8.5 psi). The 29.5 in circumference and 22 oz weight are cited from the 2009-10 rulebook edition and Wilson's official game ball spec, and are labeled as such above.
